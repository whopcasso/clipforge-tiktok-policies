ClipForge Local policies

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 October 2026

This policy describes how the locally installed ClipForge Local application handles information when its operator connects a TikTok account. Replace whopcasso@gmail.com below with an email address you control before publishing this page.

How the application works

ClipForge Local runs on the operator's own computer. The operator chooses whether to connect TikTok and whether to send a selected video for publishing. ClipForge's operator does not receive the user's video files through a ClipForge cloud service.

Information used for TikTok connection

When the user authorizes TikTok, the application receives basic account information made available by TikTok, such as the TikTok account identifier, display name, and avatar URL, plus authorization tokens needed to perform the actions the user approved.

Publishing information

When the user explicitly chooses to publish, the selected video, caption, and chosen TikTok privacy/posting settings are sent to TikTok so TikTok can process and publish the post. TikTok handles that information under TikTok's own terms and privacy policy. ClipForge Local does not publish merely because an account is connected.

Local storage and retention

TikTok connection tokens are encrypted and stored on the user's computer in ClipForge's local application-data folder. The user can disconnect the account in ClipForge, which removes its locally stored connection. The user can also delete ClipForge's local data folder to remove locally stored jobs and connection data; deleting local data does not delete posts already published to TikTok.

Sharing and analytics

The locally installed application operator does not sell TikTok account information or use it for advertising. ClipForge Local does not operate a cloud analytics service for this connection. TikTok receives information necessary to authenticate and carry out a publishing action initiated by the user.

Security

Connection tokens are encrypted at rest by the local application. The user is responsible for protecting access to their computer and local application-data folder. No method of storage is perfectly secure.

Children

ClipForge Local is not intended for children who are below the minimum age required by TikTok or applicable law.

Contact and requests

The operator can remove a TikTok connection through ClipForge or delete the local application data. Requests concerning information held by TikTok must be directed to TikTok.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when a new version is published.
